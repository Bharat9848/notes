How to lead a project
1. create a document just for yourself.
- document will have uncertainty rumours,  leads to follow, reminders, todo list
- you will always come to this when you hit a wall.
2. Write sponsors expectation and success in your words. Then have a meeting with your sponsors. Note down any ambiguity.
3. Fill out topographical map
4. Find out the why in the start of the project.
5. Find customer needs
6. Success metrics 
7. Understand and write the constraints and risk like budget, unalignment with dependent teams
